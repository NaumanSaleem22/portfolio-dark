About me

Hello! I'm Nauman

I'm a frontend-focused Full-Stack Developer based in Karachi with 5+ years of turning business requirements into fast, accessible web applications. I'm currently a Senior Frontend Developer at Symmetry Group, where I built the frontend of Zellio, an invoicing SaaS, and improve performance and WCAG accessibility across live products.

My core stack is React, Angular and TypeScript. On the backend, I build REST APIs with Node.js, Express and MongoDB, including role-based access control through custom middleware. I also customize Odoo themes and modules, and add motion and dashboards with GSAP and Chart.js.
